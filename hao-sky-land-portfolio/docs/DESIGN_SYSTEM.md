# HSL Design System

## Brand direction

**Luxury Futuristic Editorial** — architecture-led, calm, confident and technology-aware.

## Color tokens

- Obsidian: `#0B0D12`
- Obsidian raised: `#121720`
- Champagne: `#C7A56A`
- Tech Cyan: `#36D9E8`
- Ivory: `#F3F0E9`
- Muted: `#A4A8B1`

## Typography

- Archivo: display headings and oversized editorial type.
- Be Vietnam Pro: Vietnamese-first body copy and UI.
- JetBrains Mono: section markers, metadata and technical labels.

## Motion

- Slow, deliberate transitions.
- Scroll reveals use short travel distances and opacity.
- 3D particles are atmospheric, not a usability dependency.
- Mobile and reduced-motion modes reduce or disable expensive effects.
